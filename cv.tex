% ---------- Impact ----------
\sectiontitle{Selected Research Contributions}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Generalization and canonization (NeurIPS 2026):} Developed covering-number bounds comparing invariant, group-averaged, and canonized models; proved exponential complexity for lexicographic sorting versus polynomial bounds for Hilbert-curve canonization.
  \item \textbf{Expressive and stable graph learning (ICLR / LoG 2025):} Established generic expressivity guarantees for sparse geometric MPNNs under connectivity or global-rigidity assumptions; co-developed FSW-GNN, a bi-Lipschitz, 1-WL-equivalent architecture.
  \item \textbf{Weight-space learning (2026 preprint):} Studied finite-probe identifiability and universality and co-developed HIDDENPROBE, using hidden-layer responses for learning neural-network properties, with evaluations on MLPs and Transformers.
\end{itemize}
