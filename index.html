Yonatan Sverdlov

I have completed the requirements for a PhD in Mathematics at the Technion – Israel Institute of Technology, advised by Prof. Nadav Dym. My research focuses on Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), geometric deep learning, learning on model and weight spaces, expressivity, oversquashing, invariance/equivariance, and Lipschitz-stable representations. I combine a strong mathematical background with strong technical skills in machine learning research, implementation, and experimentation.

I am seeking full-time Research Scientist, Research Engineer, and Applied Scientist positions.
